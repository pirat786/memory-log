Memory Log

Privacy policy

Local recording

Memory Log stores a history of app use, window titles, active and idle time, bookmarks, and recording settings in a local SQLite database. Depending on your settings, it can also store clipboard history and system statistics on your PC. You can pause recording, exclude apps, set a retention period, and delete local history from the app's settings.

Google Drive sync

Sync is off by default. If you connect Google, Memory Log uses the drive.appdata permission to read your Drive account identifier and email address and to read and write its own hidden app data in your Google Drive. It uses the identifier to distinguish accounts and shows your email in sync settings. Windows Credential Manager holds the Google refresh token on the connected PC.

Synced records include your device name and generated identifier, window titles and activity timestamps, bookmarks and their notes, and category rules. You can choose whether to include activity recorded before you connected. Clipboard history and system statistics stay local. Devices you connect to the same account can read the synced data. Data travels over HTTPS. Memory Log applies no additional encryption to the files it writes to Google Drive.

Memory Log uses Google user data to provide sync. It has no developer-operated server that receives your Google Drive data. Memory Log does not sell or use Google user data for advertising. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting and deleting data

Disconnecting a PC stops sync on that PC, removes its local Google credential, and removes local copies of history from other devices. It leaves existing files in Drive. To remove the cloud copy, disconnect your linked PCs first and use Google Drive's Settings → Manage apps to delete Memory Log's hidden app data. You can revoke access from your Google Account connections.

Updates

Release builds connect to GitHub to check for new versions. Memory Log does not send your activity history or Google account information in update requests.
